# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Active job-seeking content creators — influencers, YouTubers, Instagrammers, TikTokers — who browse campaign opportunities, submit applications, and manage their partnership pipeline. They are goal-oriented, checking the dashboard to track application statuses, respond to brand messages, and discover new campaigns.

## Product Purpose

Brand Connect connects creators with brands for paid collaborations. For creators, it's a job-seeking and pipeline management tool. The dashboard is the daily command center: surface stats, recent activity, and quick actions so creators can spend less time managing and more time creating.

## Brand Personality

Professional, modern, energetic. Confident but not loud. The lime accent brings energy; the clean structure brings trust. Voice is direct and helpful — no fluff, no jargon.

## Anti-references

Avoid over-designed patterns: no heavy gradients, no decorative flourishes, no glassmorphism as default. No corporate blue/navy palette. No generic SaaS dashboard feel. Functional, clean, and purposeful — every element earns its place.

## Design Principles

1. **Surface the signal, hide the noise** — The dashboard should answer "what needs my attention?" at a glance. Stats, unread messages, pending applications take priority.
2. **Every pixel earns its place** — No decorative elements. Borders, backgrounds, and spacing all communicate hierarchy and state.
3. **Fast and focused** — The creator is job-seeking; the UI should feel responsive, predictable, and never slow them down.
4. **Energy through accent, not clutter** — The lime/purple palette adds life without visual weight. Use restraint.
5. **Build trust through clarity** — Statuses, actions, and next steps are always explicit. Ambiguous labels and hidden affordances erode confidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Large text ≥3:1. Visible focus states. Keyboard-navigable. Reduced-motion support for all animations.
